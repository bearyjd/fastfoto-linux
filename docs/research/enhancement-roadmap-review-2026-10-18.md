## Assessment and evidence

- **Current versus new:** narrow checks support the key distinctions: existing face-guided red-eye detection/application seam, restore-before-red-eye ordering, cropped/upright JPEG photo “originals,” PNG storage-mode kinds rather than content classification, existing document rotate/edit controls, and ordinary OCR PDF output. Hole/crease/streak assessment is correctly separated from pixel repair. `pyproject.toml:10–16` confirms GPL-3.0-or-later, Python >=3.13 and the existing OpenCV dependency. Both Flatpak dependency manifests confirm the named OpenCV/Pillow/OCRmyPDF baseline wheels and Tesseract pin.
- **Useful scope and sequence:** per-eye review/manual fallback, document Auto plus overrides, tone/color controls and manual geometry address concrete gaps. E00/E01 are bounded prerequisites rather than a new general framework; the first-release statement explicitly asks for the smallest usable source/edit contract. Dependencies allow Auto work beside photo work; heavy models, dewarp and optional batch conveniences do not block useful delivery. The synthesis matches those priorities and caveats.
- **Feasibility:** reuse of existing NumPy/OpenCV/Pillow is credible for CPU/Linux work. Source/license/runtime/Flatpak checks are correctly deferred to exact-version adoption spikes, without promising Python 3.14 compatibility for optional compiled stacks. Official [OpenCV 5 ximgproc documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/5.0/extra_modules/ximgproc.html) confirms local threshold variants; [denoise documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/5.0/main_modules/photo_denoise.html) supports the proposed first candidate. The single-`cv2` distribution rule and measured resource gates avoid an unjustified contrib/heavy-runtime commitment.
- **Important upstream claims checked:** [GEGL source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GNOME/gegl/master/operations/common-gpl3+/red-eye-removal.c) confirms operation-level GPL3+ and red-channel correction; [YuNet documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/main/models/face_detection_yunet/README.md) confirms the dynamic May-2026 export versus pinned 2023 model distinction; [OCRmyPDF 17.13.0 source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ocrmypdf/OCRmyPDF/v17.13.0/src/ocrmypdf/_pipeline.py) confirms disabled non-monochrome background removal. [BM3D authors' software page](https://webpages.tuni.fi/foi/GCF-BM3D/index.html), [GFPGAN license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TencentARC/GFPGAN/master/LICENSE), [Microsoft repository](https://github.com/microsoft/Bringing-Old-Photos-Back-to-Life), and [Epson specifications](https://epson.com/For-Home/Scanners/Photo-Scanners/FastFoto-FF-680W-Wireless-High-speed-Photo-Scanning-System/p/B11B237201) support the stated restrictions, caveats and hardware limits. These spot checks do not replace final distribution audits.
- **Evaluation and preservation:** explicit unchanged-master hashes, deterministic replay, mask locality/highlight protection, preview/export transforms, per-eye decisions, per-page mode overrides, restart/cancellation and duplex/deletion contracts are meaningful. Real/synthetic evidence is distinguished; metrics include protected content, OCR errors and subgroup failures rather than output appearance or OCR confidence alone. No fabricated benchmark, speed or accuracy result was found.

## Remaining research before implementation

No additional broad research is required to present this roadmap. E00 must still obtain rights-cleared representative real red-eye samples; SIDD and synthetic pupils cannot establish scanned-print recall. Individual feature specifications must settle numeric quality/resource budgets, exact optional-component licenses/build support, source-retention storage policy, metadata/Qt/PDF color behavior and any PDF/A validator/toolchain. These uncertainties are already disclosed and appropriately gated in the plan.
